# Product Specification

## Product
BusinessFlow AI is a low-cost SaaS for small businesses that need to manage prospects, customers, orders, recorded payments, balances, and follow-ups in one mobile-first workspace.

## MVP
The initial product is limited to:
- Organization setup and user membership.
- Prospects and customers with interaction history.
- Orders and manually recorded payments.
- Balance, advance, and remaining-payment calculations.
- Follow-up tasks and reminders.
- Commercial dashboard and CSV reports.

## Explicitly deferred
WhatsApp and email integrations, advanced AI features, automated sequences, recurring subscriptions, payment-provider activation, and public advertisements.

## Acceptance criteria
- Users can access only their organization's data.
- A customer or prospect can be created, edited, searched, and archived.
- An order has line items, totals, payment history, and an accurate remaining balance.
- A follow-up has an owner, due date, status, and related entity.
- The dashboard reflects the organization's live records.
- Core flows have automated unit, integration, and end-to-end coverage.
